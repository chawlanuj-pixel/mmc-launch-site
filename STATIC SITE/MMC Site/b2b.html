In short

B2B marketing wins business from other businesses. Sales cycles are longer, several people take part in the decision, and the goal is a qualified lead rather than a basket.

Monday Marketing Company (MMC) builds the website, creative and paid media for B2B companies in India.

  • Built for a group decisionWebsite, creative and paid media for a purchase that takes longer and involves several people.
  • Connect the CRMLeads go to a CRM and Meta's Conversions API (CAPI), so campaigns learn from the leads sales accepts.
  • Measure the qualified leadThe work is measured by the cost of a qualified lead.
Where B2B leads go missing

Five places a buyer slips away. Five services that stop it.

A B2B sale takes several people and several weeks. Each stage below is a moment where a buyer can go quiet, and the service that keeps the conversation going.

  1. 1
    Stage 1 of 5Get found

    A buyer searches for the problem and finds your competitors.

    B2B buyers research alone before they contact any supplier, and shortlist from what they find. If you are not there while the problem is live, you are not on the list.

    What we do

    We aim search and paid media at the queries buyers type when the problem is live, and build the SEO that earns visibility between campaigns.

    Search and paid media
  2. 2
    Stage 2 of 5Explain

    The site lists services but never says why you are the safer choice.

    A B2B purchase involves several people and real risk. Each needs proof they can forward: case studies, capabilities and answers to their objections.

    What we do

    We build websites, case studies and creative that answer a whole buying committee, not just the first visitor.

    Website development
  3. 3
    Stage 3 of 5Capture

    A contact page with a long form and no clear next step.

    Interested buyers rarely fill a long form for an unknown supplier. They want a demo, a call or a sample with little effort.

    What we do

    We build landing pages and short forms around one next step, such as a demo or a call, and test them so more of your traffic becomes enquiries.

    Conversion and landing pages
  4. 4
    Stage 4 of 5Qualify

    Sales says the leads are poor, and marketing cannot show why.

    Optimised on form fills, platforms learn to find the cheapest enquiries, not the ones sales accepts. Volume rises while pipeline quality falls.

    What we do

    We connect leads to your CRM and send the qualified, meeting-booked and won stages back to Meta through the Conversions API (CAPI), so campaigns learn what a good lead looks like.

    Lead generation, CRM and Meta CAPI
  5. 5
    Stage 5 of 5Follow up

    Leads go cold because nobody follows up in the first day, or after.

    B2B sales cycles are long. Most leads are not ready at first contact, and the supplier who keeps sensible contact is the one remembered when budget frees up.

    What we do

    We build retention funnels of CRM-triggered email and WhatsApp follow-ups that keep a lead warm until sales is ready.

    Retention marketing
Lead quality

How CRM and Meta CAPI make leads better.

A form fill tells Meta that someone showed interest, not that they were a good lead. Your CRM knows what happened next. The Conversions API (CAPI) carries that back to Meta.

  1. Step 1The lead arrivesA form fill, call or WhatsApp enquiry.A form fill
  2. Step 2Your CRM records what happensQualified, meeting booked, won.Qualified · Meeting · Won
  3. Step 3CAPI sends the stage to MetaA server-side event, not a browser guess.Server-side event
  4. Step 4Meta learns your best leadsIt learns from the buyers sales accepted.A clearer signal
Back to step 1The next ads aim at leads like the good ones
Without the CRM feedback

Optimised for form fills

  • Meta sees only that a form was filled.
  • It tends to find whoever fills a form cheapest.
  • Sales can end up chasing leads that never fit.
With CRM and CAPI

Optimised for qualified leads

  • Meta sees which leads sales accepted and won.
  • Spend can shift toward buyers like the ones you win.
  • The cost of a qualified lead becomes the number to watch.

Common questions

What B2B teams ask first.

What is B2B lead generation?

It is the work of bringing in enquiries from other businesses that fit who you sell to, then passing sales the ones worth a conversation. The measure that matters is the cost of a qualified lead, not the number of form fills.

How do CRM and Meta CAPI improve lead quality?

A form fill only tells Meta that someone showed interest. When your leads sit in a CRM, we send what happened next (qualified, meeting booked, won) back to Meta through the Conversions API (CAPI). Meta then learns which leads your sales team actually wants and finds more of them, instead of optimising for the cheapest form fill.

What does MMC do for B2B companies?

Websites, creative, paid media and lead generation, with a CRM and Meta CAPI set up so lead quality can be judged, and retention funnels to follow up. The tags on this page show what we run for some of the brands we work with.

How do you measure it?

On the cost of a qualified lead and how many of those become customers, not on clicks or form fills. Lead quality is judged with your sales team.

Do you work with long sales cycles?

Yes. We plan for several touchpoints and several decision-makers, and set up tracking so that lead quality can be judged, not just lead volume.

Do you only work with B2B companies?

No. We also work with D2C brands and educational institutes. Tell us what you run and we will say which approach fits.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope and, for media, on ad spend, so we quote after the audit call, once we have seen your accounts.
