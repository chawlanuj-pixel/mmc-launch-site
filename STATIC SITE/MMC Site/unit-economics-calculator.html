Questions this calculator answers

What is CM1, CM2 and CM3 in D2C?

They are the three rungs of the contribution ladder. CM1 is net revenue minus product cost. CM2 is CM1 minus fulfilment: shipping, gateway fees, packing. CM3 is CM2 minus returns and the cost of acquiring the customer. CM3 is the only one that pays for salaries, rent and profit.

How do you calculate break-even units for a D2C brand?

Divide total fixed monthly cost, including depreciation, by CM3 per order. If CM3 is negative there is no break-even volume: more orders simply enlarge the loss, and the fix has to come from price, product cost, RTO rate or CAC.

Should CAC be adjusted for RTO?

Yes. You pay to acquire every order placed, including the ones that come back undelivered. At a 25% RTO rate your effective cost per delivered order is your CAC divided by 0.75, a third higher than the figure in your ad account.
