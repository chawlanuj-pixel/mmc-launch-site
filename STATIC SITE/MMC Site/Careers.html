How we work

Six things you'd feel in your first month.

  1. 01

    Senior pay. Senior expectations.

    We hire at the top of the market and expect ownership from day one. No layered approvals, no junior cycles.

  2. 02

    No account-manager layer.

    The person doing the work talks to the client. Strategists own the relationship.

  3. 03

    Five days in studio. Two from home.

    We're an in-person studio for the work that matters. We're flexible about the rest.

  4. 04

    Own verticals, not tasks.

    We don't hand out projects, we hand out P&Ls. At four people you will own several, and all of the outcome.

  5. 05

    Write your own brief.

    Every senior here has shipped a brief unprompted in their first 90 days. That's the bar.

  6. 06

    Early enough to shape it.

    There are four of us. The person who joins next helps decide how this place works, not just what it ships.

How we hire

Four steps. Two weeks.

You'd meet every person here before anyone makes a decision. That isn't a courtesy, it's arithmetic.

  1. 01

    One page

    What you'd own, what you'd change in ninety days, what you need from us. No resume, no portal.

  2. 02

    A conversation

    Forty-five minutes with whoever runs the discipline. Mostly you asking us things.

  3. 03

    A real problem

    One paid piece of actual work from a live account, scoped to a day. We pay for it either way.

  4. 04

    The other three

    You meet everyone before anyone decides. Four people means four yeses.

Before you apply

Small is the job, not a phase we're passing through.

This will frustrate you if
  • ✕You want a defined laneAt four people the lane changes monthly. Some weeks you'll do work that isn't yours.
  • ✕You need a manager to unblock youThere isn't one. You'll have to decide, ship, and tell us afterwards.
  • ✕You want a big-company benefits sheetWe don't have one yet, and we'd rather say so than list things we can't back.
  • ✕You're waiting for process to arriveYou'd be the person writing it. That's the job, not a phase before the job.
You'd do well here if
  • ✓You've done the work yourselfNot managed someone doing it. At this size the person with the plan also builds it.
  • ✓You want the client in the roomYou'll be on the call, owning the number, without anyone translating for you.
  • ✓You'd rather own an outcome than a taskWe hand out P&Ls. You'll carry several, and all of the result.
  • ✓You want a say in what this becomesJoining at four means the shape of the company is still an open question.
