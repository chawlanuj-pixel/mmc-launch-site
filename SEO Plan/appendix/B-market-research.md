# Workstream A: Market and Search Demand (MMC SEO/AEO/GEO plan)

Access date for all SERP and source observations: 2026-09-28.

## 0. Method and hard limits (read first)

- Engine: the WebSearch tool, which is US-located only. Every SERP below is a US-located, non-personalised result set. Queries containing "India", "Mumbai", "UK" and so on bias results toward that market but are NOT localized SERPs. Real India/UK/UAE SERPs (map packs, local pack, AI Overviews, device differences) may differ.
- The tool returns titles and URLs only. It does NOT expose People Also Ask, related searches, AI Overviews, ads or SERP features. Buyer language below is therefore inferred from ranking titles/URLs, not from PAA or related-search boxes. Gap to close: manual check in a logged-out browser via VPN/SERP tool (Semrush/Ahrefs SERP features, or Google Search with gl=/hl= parameters).
- No keyword volume, CPC, KD or rank data was available. Nothing here is a volume claim. All demand evidence is "SERP presence" only (existence of many agencies targeting a phrase is a proxy that the phrase is commercially valued, not proof of volume). Verify with: Google Keyword Planner (per-country), Semrush/Ahrefs, and MMC's own Search Console.
- Agency claims found in snippets (client counts, ROAS, pricing) are competitor self-reports; they were not verified and are not used as evidence except as noted "self-reported".
- No claims are made about MMC offices or clients abroad. MMC fit is scored from what the brief states: India-based, D2C/B2B/education, services list.

## 2. Buyer search language (inferred from ranking titles, not PAA)

Observed recurring phrases (from titles): "performance marketing agency", "D2C marketing agency", "best [service] agency in [city] (2026)", "top 10 / top 13 ... agencies", "Meta ads agency", "quick commerce ads agency", "Zepto, Blinkit, Instamart, Flipkart Minutes", "Blinkit & Zepto ads cost 2026: CPC, ROAS", "WhatsApp marketing agency / automation agency", "education marketing agency / admissions / college promotion / admission lead generation", "B2B lead generation companies", "Shopify CRO agency / conversion rate optimization", "Amazon marketing agency / Amazon PPC / Amazon.ae", "Arabic and English listing optimization" (UAE), "cost / pricing / how to choose" (Mumbai D2C guide), "for D2C brands entering US" (cross-border).

Buyer-language notes:
- Year suffix "2026" and "best/top" are heavily used, indicating listicle-style comparison intent.
- Cost/pricing modifiers appear (cost guides for quick commerce; Mumbai D2C pricing), suggesting a pricing page/guide is a competitive gap.
- Vertical language differs: education uses "admissions", "enrolment", "college promotion", "school marketing"; B2B uses "lead generation", "appointment setting", "SDR"; D2C uses "ROAS", "net ROAS", "scale from launch to Rs 10Cr" (self-reported claims).
- Dubai/US queries use "DTC" (US) vs "D2C" (India/UAE). Spelling variant matters for US pages.
- To capture actual PAA/related searches: run manually or with Semrush "Keyword Magic" / AlsoAsked.
