FAQs
How is the recommended monthly budget determined?
Dozens of data points feed into the recommended monthly budget, including our decades of experience serving thousands of businesses across hundreds of industries.
In addition, we use our AI-powered forecasting technology to calculate the level of spend and allocation that businesses would need (based on their industry, business model, size, marketing reach, and marketing maturity) to reach their growth goal.
Besides internal data, our marketing budget calculator also considers research from trusted third-party firms, like Gartner and Deloitte.
Together, these data points help us provide businesses with accurate estimates for an effective (and personalized) monthly spend for digital marketing.
How do you calculate a marketing budget?
Start with your annual revenue, then factor in your industry, business model, company size, and growth goal — these determine what percentage of revenue you should set aside and how to split it across channels like SEO, PPC, content, and email. Our marketing budget calculator does this automatically: enter those details above and get a recommended monthly budget with a suggested channel breakdown.
How much should you budget for marketing?
Most companies spend between 5-12% of revenue on marketing, though the right number depends on your industry and growth goals. Use our marketing budget calculator above to get a personalized estimate based on your specific business.
What if I don’t know the answer to some of the questions?
If you don’t know the answer to some of the calculator’s questions, like the average sales cycle length or the company’s growth goal, take a guess or run the calculator multiple times to build a range of results that can help inform your marketing budget planning.
Are the budget allocations based on internal or outsourced marketing management?
The budget allocations use a mixed model to consider in-house vs. outsourced marketing management. A more conservative budget, for example, will likely rely on an in-house program while a more aggressive one will have the means to support an agency partnership.
What do the marketing channel budget allocations include?
The marketing channel budget allocations include:
Strategy development
Strategy management
Professional services
They do not include (in the case of digital advertising) ad spend.
Is it still accurate to allocate marketing spend based on business revenue?
Allocating marketing spend based on business revenue serves as a starting point for calculating your marketing budget. However, how much a business spends on marketing should consider other factors, like:
Industry, which influences competition across organic and paid channels
Marketing maturity, which shapes the strategy of different channels (and the amount of runway businesses have to grow)
Sales cycle length, which impacts how soon businesses see the results from marketing efforts
Growth goals, which significantly influence how much an organization allocates to its marketing efforts.
The above factors are all included in our marketing budget calculator in order to provide organizations with a more informed estimate on how much to spend and where.
How should I use this marketing budget calculator?
Use this marketing budget calculator as a research device for:
Estimating overall spend based on your industry, business model, size, and other factors unique to you
Understanding budget allocations
Determining different spend tiers, like conservative, moderate, and aggressive
Forecasting different scenarios, like spending heavier in one strategy or more aggressively overall
What do the conservative, moderate, and aggressive budget recommendations mean?
The conservative, moderate, and aggressive budget models represent different approaches to managing your marketing spend while achieving your growth goals:
Model Purpose
Conservative Prioritizes incremental growth while respecting budget or buy-in limitations.
Moderate Prioritizes balanced growth across digital marketing channels.
Aggressive Prioritizes expedited growth across channels while allocating the most funds to experimentation.
What if my industry isn’t listed in the calculator?
First, share your feedback with us. Then, select the most comparable industry. For example, if operating in the home services industry with a focus on remodeling, consider selecting “Construction / Trades” as an alternative option.
How often should I recalculate my marketing budget?
Businesses should recalculate marketing spend when:
Providers implement price increases, whether for third-party tools or agency services
Organizations shift to a more aggressive growth goal (which will require additional spend)
Companies switch to targeting a new market or audience
Businesses see a downturn in company performance, which could indicate a need to change the overall budget or budget allocations
By default, organizations tend to recalculate their marketing budgets annually. The above, however, outlines events that can trigger a recalculation.

What is readability score?
Readability score algorithms roughly estimate the level of education someone would need in order to read a passage of text easily.
There are several algorithms available for gauging readability score, and they use factors such as sentence length, syllable count, and the percentage of multi-syllable words in order to determine readability score.
Most readability scores are comprised of two factors. The first factor relates to sentence structure, and typically measures the average number of words per sentence. Readability indices also take into account word structure and are generally based on the average number of syllables per word or the proportion of easy words determined by referencing a word list.
For the general public, your text should reflect a readability of about 8th grade.
How can I improve my readability score?
Now that you understand the importance of readability, we’ll take a look at a checklist for improving the readability of your content.The next time you add a new page to your website, consider these tips:
Limit the length of your sentences and paragraphs
Most times, readers want to be able to easily skim over the information contained on websites and pick out the main points.
In order for them to be able to do this, you need to keep your sentences and paragraphs relatively short. For the web, optimal line length is between 50-60 characters. And you should generally limit paragraphs to around three sentences.
This will help you to hold the attention of readers and keep them engaged in your content.
Be concise
Keep your writing concise and avoid flowery language that adds unnecessary bulk to your writing.
Readers don’t want to sort through a mountain of unnecessary text. Instead, they should be able to easily pick up on the main points of your writing.
By eliminating redundancy and getting right to the point, you can improve the readability of your writing.
Avoid technical language
Unless you’re writing to a niche group, avoid using jargon in your content.
This can confuse writers, and they will likely navigate away from your website and search for information that is easier to understand.
Though you want to position yourself as a leader in your field, you don’t need to use fancy terminology. In fact, people will respect you more if you present information in a way that is easy for them to digest and understand.
Use lists
Bulleted lists are a great way to present information in a way that is easy to understand. They also make the information stand out on a page and help readers grasp what you’re trying to convey. Lists can help people stay focused on your writing by highlighting important information in a way that is easy for them to comprehend.

How does a page’s readability affect AI search visibility?
A page’s readability score can affect AI search visibility significantly because LLMs like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google’s AI Overviews prefer clear and concise statements.
When possible, aim for a Flesch Reading Ease score between 60-70.
How should I write for answer engines like ChatGPT?
Individuals and businesses can write for answer engines by:
Opening sections using the inverted pyramid, which provides an immediate answer to the user’s or section’s question
Structuring content with lists, tables, and headings
Including citable information, like data points, quotes, and first-hand experiences
Limiting vague pronouns, like replacing “this digital marketing agency…” for “WebFX”
Altogether, the above approaches create better content for both users and answer engines as the content is easy-to-read, memorable, and helpful.
