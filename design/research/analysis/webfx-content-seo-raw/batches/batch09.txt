1. Facebook
MAU: 3 billion
Platform type: General-purpose social network 
Content formats: Text posts, images, videos, stories, carousels, polls, live streams, Facebook Groups, and product listings 
Ideal for: eCommerce stores, retail brands, non-profits, B2C companies, educators, and local service businesses 
Facebook offers something for everyone, including diverse advertising opportunities for the 89% of global marketers who already use the platform. It’s the largest social media platform globally and among the biggest local business directories, which makes it a crucial part of any successful social media marketing campaign. 
Businesses can leverage Facebook as their all-in-one marketing, advertising, and communications platform. How? You can use Facebook to build brand awareness, run highly targeted ads, manage customer interactions, and sell products and services.
So what makes Facebook the best social media platform for small businesses?
It supports a wide variety of content formats.
It facilitates deep community building and customer engagement through features like Facebook Messenger and Facebook Groups.
It has a wide demographic range that connects you with younger and older consumers.
It includes many business-friendly features, including messenger automation, appointment scheduling, and access to detailed analytics.
It allows selling products and services right on the app through catalog ads, product tagging, and Facebook shops.
Unfortunately, Facebook algorithms limit your organic reach. However, you can bypass this hurdle with paid ads through Meta Ads Manager, which helps you drive paid traffic immediately. 
2. YouTube
MAU: 2.5 billion
Platform type: Video sharing platform
Content formats: Long form and short form videos, livestreams, shorts, community polls, images, and text
Ideal for: B2C, B2B, e-commerce, tech companies, educators, influencers, SaaS brands
YouTube is the quintessential video marketing platform with a huge audience and rapidly growing content formats. It’s the best platform for your business if you’re looking to educate your audience through product reviews, instructional videos, case studies, and other behind-the-scenes content for your brand.
YouTube has the potential to bolster your business growth as it can be viewed as a search engine, content hub, and reliable marketing channel. There are many reasons to choose YouTube as one of your preferred marketing channels. Here are some:
YouTube videos appear in Google search results, improving your search visibility. 
Combine that with YouTube ads and Shorts, and you’ve got plenty of room for potential with a YouTube marketing campaign.
YouTube videos have a long shelf life and continue driving traffic and leads years after publishing.
YouTube Studio offers rich analytics that comprise views, watch time, engagement rates, and demographics.
On the flip side, YouTube may not be your ideal marketing platform if you’re looking for a platform that drives instant results, it takes time to drive revenue. Additionally, B2B brands with a niche audience may not gain traction from the platform unless they invest heavily in the quality of your videos.
3. Instagram
MAU: 2 billion
Platform type: Visual-first social network
Content formats: Single and carousel photos, reels, stories, live videos, notes, product tags
Ideal for: B2C businesses in a wide variety of industries, including fashion, beauty, lifestyle, food, travel, fitness, wellness, ecommerce, and local brands
Instagram is a great platform for businesses and marketers just starting out with social media marketing because it’s home to a variety of users, from teens and Gen Zs to millennials. With plenty of content formats available, it’s easy to get started and get noticed with Instagram for business.
With Instagram marketing, businesses like yours can appeal to users with creative storytelling and unforgettable aesthetics. Instagram is also a great platform if you’re looking to build community, enhance brand awareness and drive business sales through engaging visual content.
What’s so special about Instagram for business?
As part of the Meta Platforms family, Instagram integrates with Facebook, Threads, WhatsApp, and more to facilitate a seamless social media marketing strategy.
It provides a visual-first platform for showcasing your brand personality, values, offerings, and experiences.
Instagram drives the highest organic engagement rates across all social media platforms.
It supports a built-in shopping experience through product tagging, Instagram shops, and in-app checkout. 
It’s popular for influencer marketing, which enables you to grow your reach across a wider audience.
It has strong community features that foster authentic relationships with followers, like stories, polls, DMs, and livestreams.
While you may be fortunate to increase your organic reach fast via Instagram, it’s inconsistent due to its dependence on the algorithm and the platform’s ranking system. It also limits link sharing on individual posts and not ideal for brands whose marketing efforts are heavily dependent on text.
4. WhatsApp
MAU: 2 billion
Platform type: Social messaging app
Content formats: Text messages, voice messages, images, videos, documents, story-style updates, business catalogs, product listings, broadcast list, group chats, click-to-click chats
Ideal for: Local businesses, e-commerce businesses, and service-based businesses, like healthcare facilities, logistic companies, and travel agencies
WhatsApp is considered one of the best social media platforms for small businesses because it allows you to streamline communication between you and your customers. It’s an organized app, with a user-friendly interface, that you can rely on to improve customer service by enabling real-time customer support.
With continuous innovations, WhatsApp is becoming increasingly reliable for personalizing business marketing, managing community updates, and showcasing products. Other use cases of WhatsApp for businesses include broadcasting limited-time offers and updating customer orders.
Here are some reasons why it’s worth having WhatsApp as part of your social media marketing strategy:
It provides faster and more direct access to customers compared to other outreach methods like email marketing.
It facilitates automated messaging with bots and pre-written templates for messages, frequently asked questions (FAQs), and confirmations.
The end-to-end encryption feature helps to build trust with customers.
It connects directly to Facebook and Instagram ads for instant messaging.
WhatsApp limits your reach because it’s not a discovery platform and limits customer reach without consent by enabling them to block and report your page. It’s also ideal for mobile, and may limit businesses that rely heavily on desktop workflows. 
5. TikTok
MAU: 1.5 billion
Platform type: Short-form video entertainment and discovery
Content formats: Vertical short-form videos, stories, livestreams, duets, stitches, polls, stickers, and interactive filters
Ideal for: B2C brands, including lifestyle, fashion, food, fitness, wellness, entertainment, tech gadgets, eCommerce, and lifestyle businesses 
In light of recent discussions about what a potential TikTok ban means for marketing, it’s important to recognize that the platform currently hosts diverse audiences, making it a valuable space for video content creators. It’s the ideal platform for entertaining and engaging your audience with creative, relatable, funny, and inspiring videos. 
TikTok has the potential to help you connect with new audiences organically or through ad placements to nurture leads, increase brand awareness, and even sell products! If your target audience consists mainly of trend-aware and visual-first Gen Zs and millennials, then you’re missing out on lots of traffic if you’re not utilizing TikTok.
You can unlock many new marketing opportunities with TikTok in the following ways:
The TikTok algorithm prioritizes discovery over follower count, giving brands of all sizes an equal opportunity to grow organically or go viral.
TikTok users spend an average of 90 minutes daily on the app, promising high engagement rates.
You can repurpose TikTok content for use on other platforms, like Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
Product tags and TikTok shops allow you to sell your products and services directly on the app.
It offers many opportunities for visibility and discoverability, including challenges, viral hashtags, meme formats, and trending audio.
If you can’t keep up with the high content output, TikTok may not be for your business. Additionally, TikTok doesn’t work well for B2B companies because the content is more creative and targeted at consumers.
6. Reddit
MAU: 1.3 billion
Platform type: Forum-based discussion platform 
Content formats: Text posts, images, videos, and polls
Ideal for: Niche communities, brands looking to build credibility, startups, tech, gaming and SaaS brands
Reddit is an online community platform built up of over 100,000 subreddits or forums, dedicated to topics ranging from plant care to discussing the latest episodes of popular TV shows. It’s a valuable business communication platform due to its community-driven nature that fosters user trust and engagement. 
The platform allows businesses access to niche audiences segmented by different interests and demographics, allowing you to reach users genuinely interested in specific products and services. You can also leverage Reddit to collect feedback because users on the platform confidently share their authentic experiences, insights, and opinions. 
Here are some top reasons why Reddit is the best social media for business marketing:
There’s a Subreddit for almost anything, so you’re likely to find various segments of your audience hanging around one forum or another. 
Reddit posts and discussions can show up in Google search results, so it could add another layer of visibility to your search engine optimization (SEO) efforts.
It offers an opportunity for content discovery as you monitor questions, discussions, and trending topics relating to your offerings and industry. 
Reddit Ads allows you to run ultra-targeted marketing campaigns segmented by Subreddits, location, and interests. 
Reddit may not be the right platform if you operate a business in any of the following highly-regulated industries: finance, legal, or health. It’s also not for you if your brand is overly promotional because Reddit users like to downvote blatant marketing. 
7. WeChat
MAU: 1.3 billion 
Platform type: All-in-one super app
Content formats: Text and voice messages, videos, images, mini-programs, and livestreams 
Ideal for: Brands targeting the Asian or Chinese market, eCommerce companies, retailers, and luxury product brands
WeChat is the most popular social media app in China and other parts of Asia. WeChat started out as a simple messaging platform and has since evolved into an all-in-one app that allows for messaging, calls, shopping, reservations, bookings for taxis, and more!
Because popular social media platforms like Facebook, X, and YouTube are banned in China, advertising on WeChat is a great alternative for brands looking to expand their reach in China and tap into Asia’s diverse audience.
Businesses can use WeChat, which provides an integrated business ecosystem that supports the creation of brand pages, publishing content, engaging users, and processing payments. Some of the most appealing features of WeChat include:
Get consistent brand presence with WeChat Official Accounts that double as mini websites and email newsletters
Seamless eCommerce integration with WeChat Stores and WeChat Pay
Custom in-apps provide access to books, games, and access to many services 
WeChat Ads unlocks highly targeted advertising and reach to precise demographics 
If you have no intention of targeting audiences in China or Asia, WeChat will not drive results for you. 
8. LinkedIn 
MAU: 1 billion
Platform type: Professional networking and content platform 
Content formats: Articles, text posts, images, infographics, shot and long form videos, live streams, newsletters, document uploads, newsletters, polls, and surveys 
Ideal for: B2B companies, consultants, professional service providers, recruiters, tech firms, and SaaS businesses 
If you’re a business-to-business (B2B) company, LinkedIn is the ultimate social media platform. With over 67 million companies and organizations, it’s no wonder that 64% of global marketers use LinkedIn as part of their social media strategy.
Linked allows you to combine organic content and paid ad opportunities to create targeted marketing campaigns that effectively reach your most valuable audiences. B2B businesses should leverage LinkedIn to connect with your niche audiences, push thought leadership content, and practice employee advocacy. 
Other unique features of LinkedIn that would make it a good fit for your business include:
The LinkedIn algorithm rewards consistency and meaningful engagement, which boosts your reach in the short and long term.
LinkedIn, being a professional networking platform, can help you build trust, credibility, and authority. 
You can leverage LinkedIn ads and lead generation forms to drive qualified prospects for B2B campaigns. 
It provides direct access to company decision-makers and C-suite executives.
LinkedIn is not reliable for B2C companies, as it may not effectively reach consumers. It also restricts you to using a formal tone, which doesn’t suit creative and edgy brands and has slower engagement rates compared to other platforms like Instagram and TikTok.
9. Telegram 
MAU: 950 million
Platform type: Messaging app with automation, broadcasting, and community management features
Content formats: Text messages, images, videos, audio, chatbots, voice, and video calls
Ideal for: Tech-savvy, edtech, crypto, finance, gaming, and SaaS businesses
Telegram is a free messaging app that doesn’t limit media sizes and is available for mobile devices and desktop computers. It’s an ideal platform for businesses looking to enable fast, secure, and large-scale communication.
You can also depend on Telegram to build powerful communities, nurture leads, and deliver content at scale. End-to-end encryption and cloud storage of files and messages allows, niche brands use the app to provide one-on-one customer support.
Businesses can leverage Telegram for a number of uses, including:
Deploying chatbots: Telegram allows businesses to easily set up chatbots with interactive interfaces and multilingual support to automate marketing or streamline customer interactions. 
Building interactive groups: On Telegram, you can create groups of up to 200,000 members, which you can use for community-building, engaging in discussions, and hosting interactive Q&A sessions. 
Offering paid content: Professional service businesses like course creators and financial advisors can leverage Telegram to sell exclusive content, build memberships, and gate-keep content. 
Messaging automation: Telegram’s Bot APIs allow sending targeted messages, personalized responses, greetings, and quick replies 
Multi-device support: Employees can access their chats on multiple devices simultaneously to facilitate real-time collaboration from different locations.
The major limitation of Telegram is that you must drive traffic to your channel from other channels, like your website or email list, because it lacks a native search or explore function. It also lacks a built-in ad platform.
10. Snapchat
MAU: 850 million
Platform type: Spontaneous messaging and short-form multimedia sharing
Content formats: Snaps, stories, spotlights, ads
Ideal for: Beauty, fashion, tech, music, entertainment, lifestyle, and local businesses targeting younger audiences aged between 13 and 34 
Launched in 2011, Snapchat laid the foundation for the rise of short-form, vertical video content that lasts 24 hours. Known as “snaps,” content on the platform is shared between friends through private chats and public stories. Users can also explore content from other creators through 
Snapchat is the right platform for businesses marketing to teen audiences through informal, authentic, and visually appealing content. Snapchat media expire in 24 hours for one-on-one chats and after seven days for group chats.
The following Snapchat features make the platform ideal for business communication and collaboration: 
Ephemeral (temporary) content that ensures conversations stay light, fast, and fleeting. 
Direct-to-consumer connections via DMs, stories, and behind-the-scenes content. 
Mobile-only audience provides access to an ever-expanding mobile-first customer base.
Snapchat ads and influencer collaborations extend your brand’s reach.
AR filters, geofilters, and lenses enable the creation of interactive content.
Sadly, the limited content lifespan on Snapchat reduces the long-term value. Additionally, it limits your reach to audiences under 25 years, who make up 48% of Snapchat’s active users. 
11. X (formerly Twitter)
MAU: 586 million
Platform type: Microblogging platform and digital town square
Content formats: Character-limited text posts, threads, images, videos, live audio, polls, community posts, newsletters
Ideal for: B2B brands, media outlets, multinational corporations, non-profit organizations, government, and service-based organizations
X is a popular microblogging platform that thrives on real-time and time-sensitive interactions, short-form updates, and trending topics. Brands can use X to update their audiences on new products and services, or include it as part of their customer service efforts. 
Many social media users leverage X to offer feedback, especially when they need to escalate an issue or can’t seem to conveniently reach your business. So, you should keep an eye out for mentions and feedback on X and offer empathetic and professional responses to prevent reputational damage. 
There are numerous features of X that make it a unique marketing or advertising platform for businesses, like:
Its text-first emphasis makes it ideal for thought leadership and hot takes
It emphasizes the sharing of time-sensitive content, like company-wide updates, breaking news, and live commentary.
It’s a powerful customer service hub that allows engagement through comments, reposts, and quite tweets.
It provides access to an engaged audience already discussing trending topics they’re passionate about
While you can market your business for free on X, you can expect a fast decline in organic traffic due to constant algorithm changes and noise. Your posts also have short visibility windows, often a few minutes or hours, and you risk reputational damage from one viral post offering negative feedback.
12. Pinterest 
MAU: 537 million
Platform type: Visual discovery and bookmarking platform
Content formats: Infographics, images, carousels, idea pins, and short videos
Ideal for: eCommerce, lifestyle, food, fashion, home decor, travel, beauty, and DIY brands
Pinterest is a visual-first platform that Internet users with high purchase intent run to for inspiration, practical solutions, and ideas for food, travel, and fashion. The Pinterest feeds have a longer shelf life, meaning their relevance does not fade months after posting.
Pinterest is a search engine and social media platform that drives long-term website traffic and boosts sales. You can use it to showcase products and ideas, using striking visuals to create a highly engaged audience. 
The following are some benefits of using Pinterest for Businesses:
Great for showcasing and selling products with product collections and shoppable pins
Maximizes reach with opportunities to repurpose content, like blog posts, Instagram posts, or blog posts
Optimized Pinterest content offers an SEO boost that helps your content rank on Google, too
Enable brand discovery with the ability to link directly to your website and product pages
It’s not the platform for B2B businesses or brands in highly technical industries with low visual appeal. You should also avoid Pinterest if you’re hoping to go viral because it’s more supportive of long-term discovery. 
13. Threads
MAU: 275 million
Platform type: Text-first microblogging platform
Content formats: Text posts, images, videos, and GIFs
Ideal for: Marketing and social media agencies, SaaS companies, news outlets, beauty, and fashion brands
As Instagram’s answer to X (Twitter), Threads offers another online space for people to connect via conversation. It offers a clean and integrated microblogging experience for businesses already using Facebook and Instagram for marketing purposes.
You can use Threads to connect with your audience, foster trust, and build tight-knit communities. Threads can help you humanize your brand and establish a more authentic and unfiltered voice. Your business will enjoy using Threads because:
It integrates with Meta platforms to facilitate cross-promotion.
It offers more organic visibility for early adopters because it’s still in the growth phase 
It favors authentic content, interactions, and engagement to help expand your reach 
It complements other social media efforts to help build deeper connections 
For businesses testing newer text-based channels, reviewing Threads benchmarks can help determine whether the platform is contributing meaningfully to engagement, community growth, and brand visibility.
Threads will limit your access to deep analytics because it leads to robust ad tools. Additionally, the platform lacks advertising options, which limits your reach to an organic audience only. 
14. Discord
MAU: 154 million
Platform type: Online community-building platform
Content formats: Texts, voice and video chats, screen sharing, file sharing, and integrations
Ideal for: Tech-savvy audiences, brands in need of tight-knit communities, creator-driven businesses, gaming, SaaS, and NFT brands
Discord was originally designed as an online community platform for gamers. Today, it’s being adopted by businesses in a wide variety of industries, including educational institutions, fintech companies, and startups.
On Discord, users can communicate with other people in real time via voice and text chats, create servers, and join different communities. It’s an easy-to-use, customizable platform meant to connect business owners with people all around the Internet who share interests, products, and services. 
Here are a few reasons why your business will stand out when you use Discord:
Create private, branded communities or servers around your offerings, niche interests, projects, and departments 
Allows the use of real-time voice and video messaging 
Basic features are free, making it a cost-effective communication tool 
Ideal platforms for hosting online events like product reveals, Q&A sessions, workshops, and webinars
Get access to detailed analytics and insights to track key metrics, like message frequency and user engagement
If your business services an older audience that’s not tech-savvy, who may find the interface challenging to navigate.
15. Nextdoor
MAU: 88+ million
Platform type: Hyper-localized social networking platform
Content formats: Text, images, sponsored ads 
Ideal for: Local, service-based, and community-oriented businesses
Are you running a hyper-localized or community-focused business targeting one specific neighborhood? Then, Nextdoor will help you connect with your audience and stay up-to-date on relevant neighborhood updates. 
To make use of Nextdoor, you need to claim your business page, then use it to connect with potential customers in your immediate vicinity. Being active on Nextdoor could help you create rapport with your local community, driving organic recommendations and word-of-mouth marketing.
Nextdoor has a few unique features that stand out and make it the best social media platform for businesses of all sizes:
Free business pages that you can use them to showcase your services and gather recommendations and reviews
Allows targeted advertisements to specific neighborhoods and audience demographics
Offers numerous community engagement opportunities, including a chance to participate in neighborhood discussions and access to customer reviews 
Unfortunately, Nextdoor’s reach is limited beyond your local area, and engagement rates are solely dependent on the neighborhood. 
16. Twitch 
MAU: 7.2 million 
Platform type: Live streaming platform 
Content formats: Live streams, video-on-demand (VOD), subscriber-only content, clips, live chats
Ideal for: Tech, eCommerce, lifestyle, entertainment, media, and gaming brands
Twitch is a game-streaming platform that lets users tune into live streams and have real-time conversations with other viewers and streamers. It provides you with access to niche audiences and an opportunity to build a sense of camaraderie around your channel.
Twitch is the perfect social media platform for businesses with strong personalities and a community-driven branding strategy. The platform offers unique opportunities for real-time engagement, influencer marketing, and brand awareness. 
You can use Twitch to host live product launches or reviews, live entertainment sessions, behind-the-scenes streams, and interactive tutorials or classes. The platform has numerous perks that you can leverage for lead generation:
Advertising ops: Twitch unlocks diverse advertising opportunities with features, like display ads, pre-roll ads, and sponsored streams. 
Niche targeting: Businesses targeting Gen Z and millennial markets gain access to engaged audiences interested in gaming, tech, and lifestyle products and services.
Ripe for influencer marketing: Partner with relevant Twitch streamers to showcase your products or services live, allowing you to reach a dedicated fan base. 
Discoverability: A huge selling point for Twitch is that the platform has discoverability features, such as recommended streams and categories, which help you reach new audiences.
On the flip side, with Twitch, you cannot target older and non-tech-savvy audiences who may be unaware of the platform and unfamiliar with the user interface. It’s also not the platform if you’re targeting B2B corporate buyers because it’s too informal and more suitable for consumer-focused brands.

Should You Outsource Your Social Media Marketing?
Published: Jan 1, 2026
7 min. read
Author
Social media marketing is an excellent way to build brand awareness, reduce client churn, and improve customer satisfaction — but it takes time and effort. So, if you’re wondering if you should outsource your social media marketing, keep reading for advice and tips!
When should you outsource your social media?
There are many reasons that outsourcing your social media campaigns can help your business. If any of the following statements are true about your company’s social strategy, you may want to consider working with an outside agency.
1. You don’t have the time to commit to a social media campaign
Social media marketing is a time commitment. In order to see results, your business needs to allocate enough time for several tasks, including customer engagement, research, content creation, and performance analysis.
Without all of these steps, your social media marketing can fall flat, and the time you put in can easily be wasted.
How much time you spend marketing your business on social media is dependent on how many platforms you are marketing on and how you choose to market on them.
2. It’s weighing down your employees
How do you handle your company’s social media? Do you have it rationed to different employees that are already busy?
If the answer is yes, outsourcing is a great option.
Assigning social media marketing to numerous employees as “side work” won’t get you the best results — mostly because it is just a side job. This could also mean that your employees that are already working on other tasks aren’t dedicating as much time into their social channels as they should be in order to generate results.
If you’re delegating your social media marketing strategy to employees that are already busy, it’s likely not getting the effort that it should to yield the most beneficial results for your company.
3. Social media marketing isn’t a priority
If you want the most from your social media marketing, it should be a priority for your business.
You should aim to post on nearly all of your social media accounts daily, and if that isn’t a part of your current schedule, social media might not be high enough on your priority list.
There is also an art to what time of day you post on social media. If you don’t have a clear strategy, it’s likely that you aren’t posting at optimal times for engagement – and rather at the optimal time for your schedule.
If you find it difficult to keep up with creating enough content and managing each of your social media accounts, you should consider outsourcing.
4. You aren’t getting the results you want
If you’re simply not getting the results you desire from your current social media marketing, it might be because you’re spread too thin.
In order to attract new customers for your business, you should give your social media marketing strategy the same amount of attention that you give to all your other marketing channels. Simply having social media profiles on different channels isn’t enough to generate results.
How much does outsourcing social media marketing cost?
It’s hard to pinpoint an exact cost for outsourcing your social media marketing.
The cost depends on several factors, including:
The marketing firm you hire
How many channels you want to market on
How often you want to post
Whether you want the agency to create site content to go with social posts
That being said, most agencies charge both an initial and month fee for their services.
Of course, pricing depends on the one you choose to hire for your social media marketing needs. And in many cases, if you’re thinking about outsourcing your social media, it’s better to outsource multiple channels since most agencies will bundle for a cheaper price.

Which platforms can you use social media outsourcing for?
Here are a few of the most popular social media platforms for marketing and how you can advertise your business on each:
Facebook
Facebook is one of the most popular social media platforms for marketing. As a result, it drives almost 25 percent of social referral traffic.
If your company uses Facebook as one of its marketing platforms, you can try many different advertising techniques.
You can write updates about your business that offer insight into your daily specials or upcoming events, post pictures of your storefront and products, and even share blogs straight from your website.
Posting has a lot of power because it gives you the ability to your followers and create conversation. All of your posts give your followers the opportunity to comment, which can blossom into customer to customer conversation about your company and products. This also gives you an opportunity to join the conversation.
Just be sure you monitor your comments and respond to any questions in a timely manner.
You can also use the platform to run paid ads for your business in users’ feeds.
Facebook ads are effective because you can target your audience based on age, gender, interests, and other information. This allows you to create highly targeted ads for your most valuable potential customers.
And the best part is, you only pay for the ad when it’s clicked on. (Unless you’re running Facebook ads based on impressions.)
Twitter
Twitter is another great social media platform for marketing, but requires a different approach than Facebook.
In order to market your business on Twitter, you have to find a way to interest potential customers with a limited amount of characters. However, short and sweet definitely has its benefits.
Tweets limit you to a short 140 characters, which forces you to include only the bare minimum of what you want to convey, as well as a link to additional information.
You can also include pictures, videos, and gifs in your tweets, which can help attract attention to your account and traffic to your website.
Your followers can retweet your tweets, which can spread your company’s name to more Twitter users.
You can also market your business on Twitter using paid ads, which you can target by demographic.
Instagram
Instagram is a different kind of social media platform than Facebook and Twitter.
Where the first two platforms concentrate more on written content, Instagram is photo-based. Each post must include a photo or short video, and the only written content is in captions.
You can use Instagram to post images of anything related to your company—things like behind-the-scenes shots, new products, short snippets of a customer testimonial, and more.
You can also run paid advertisements on Instagram, which much like Facebook and Twitter, allow you to target your audience so that you market to your most valuable potential customers.
Pinterest
Pinterest is just 5 years old, but is already the third most popular social network in the US. Given its growing user base, marketing on the platform is becoming increasingly popular.
This platform is a place for “pinning” online content that other users can see. You can pin content like products, original graphics, and blog posts, and include images to make your followers want to learn more. It’s also helpful to “repin” other people’s posts.
There are also PPC ads available for Pinterest, called promoted pins, which allows you to decide the demographic that sees your ads and only pay when they’re clicked.
LinkedIn
You may not initially consider LinkedIn as one of the best social media platforms to market your business, but you should. Especially because it can drive 4 times more visitors to your homepage than Facebook and Twitter.
If you’re looking to market your business on LinkedIn, it’s better to post long-form articles rather than short posts. As a professional platform, long reads and articles are better suited to its user base.

What is web design outsourcing?
Web design outsourcing involves hiring a freelancer or agency to do design tasks for your website. The requirements can range from redesigning your existing site and improving its user experience (UX) to creating a new design concept.
5 considerations when you outsource web design
Should you outsource web design or do it in-house? The answer to this question varies per business.
Some businesses will benefit from doing web design in-house, while it’s more practical for other companies to outsource this task to experts. Before tapping the help of web design agencies or freelancers, review your needs and these considerations:
1. Requirements
Start by defining your web design project’s scope.
Identify the number of pages you’ll publish. Then, determine the website features you’ll need, such as ecommerce functionality or point-of-sale system.
Having a list of requirements gets you on the right track of whether you must outsource or not.
2. Expertise
Does your in-house team have the expertise to design your website? If your team has never designed a website, you can outsource the task to trusted experts.
If your team has experience designing websites, consider doing it in-house. However, you must take into account other factors, including their workload and focus areas, bringing us to the next item you must consider.
3. Core business
Your marketing team has the skills and experience to design your website. However, before doing it in-house, think about your core business and your team’s focus areas.
If your in-house, experienced web designer is your team’s dedicated branding professional, they may lose their focus on their primary tasks. In such cases, it makes sense to outsource web design so your team member can focus on branding.
4. Budget
Next, consider the cost of outsourcing web design. Does your marketing budget allow for you to hire an agency or freelancer to design your website?
5. Timeline
Identify your deadline for your web design project. Remember that the more pages and features your website will have, the more time it may take for a freelancer or agency to accomplish it.
In-house vs. outsourced web design
Deciding whether you’ll outsource web design or hire an in-house web design team is a crucial decision to make. Here’s a summary of their advantages and disadvantages:
 IN-HOUSE WEB DESIGN OUTSOURCED WEB DESIGN
Brand familiarity An in-house web design team is typically familiar with your business and offerings An external contractor or agency may not be as familiar with your brand as an in-house team member
Communication Collaboration is easier with an in-house design team Communication barriers such as time zone and language differences
Support Expect immediate support for urgent web design needs from your in-house team Some agencies and freelancers can’t provide immediate support for urgent design needs
Cost Hiring an in-house web design team means you’ll spend on your team members’ salaries, benefits, and training Outsourcing web design is more cost-efficient since you’ll only pay for the services you need
Experience and skills In-house web designers may not have the extensive experience, skills, and up-to-date training of agencies and freelancers Freelancers and agencies have a broad experience in different industries. They also invest in upskilling to ensure they remain competitive.
Scalability Scaling up your efforts with an in-house team can be more challenging, as you need to hire and train new team members. Agencies and freelancers are agile and can scale up and down their efforts depending on your needs
In-house web design: pros and cons
Hiring internal team members for your website design and development has its share of pros and cons. Here are its benefits and drawbacks:
Pros
Brand familiarity: In-house web design teams are typically familiar with your business, products or services, and even history.
Communication: Your in-house web designer can easily collaborate with other team members, such as your sales, product development, and customer support.
Support: You can expect immediate support for your web design needs because your in-house designer is dedicated to your business.
Cons
Cost: Maintaining an in-house team of designers can be costly. Their payroll, benefits, equipment, and training are all charged to your business.
Experience and skills: Your in-house web designer may know your brand inside and out, but their skills and experience may not be as extensive as those of a freelancer or agency.
Scalability and flexibility: Scaling up your web design efforts with an in-house team may be challenging. You may need to hire new team members, which takes time.
Outsourced web design: Pros and cons
Hiring a freelancer or an agency for your website design needs enables you to focus on running your business. That’s not to say it’s the perfect solution for all businesses.
Analyze your needs and review the pros and cons of outsourcing website design:
Pros
Cost: Outsourcing web design can be less costly for your business. You won’t have to spend on the designers’ equipment and upskilling.
Fresh perspective: While outsourced website designers may not be as familiar with your business as your in-house team members, they can offer fresh perspectives. Provide them with a detailed brief of your web design project, and they can potentially bring new ideas for your marketing.
Experience and skills: When you outsource web design to an agency, you get access to several experts and designers who have various skills and experiences in different industries. Even freelance website designers may have extensive experience since they typically come from agencies and have designed for various brands.
Scalability: If you need to scale up your web design efforts because of market conditions or previously unforeseen circumstances, outsourcing offers you the flexibility to address new design projects. You won’t have to take time to hire and train new employees.
Cons
Brand familiarity: Freelancers and agencies may not be as familiar with your brand as your in-house team.
Potential communication barriers: You may face communication barriers with outsourced talent since they may be in a different time zone. They may even speak a different language.
Less direct management: When you outsource your web design, you may have less control over the team working on your designs. You may also not be able to oversee their design processes.
Support: While agencies and freelancers must provide you with excellent support, your in-house web designers can drop their other tasks and focus on your urgent needs.

Let’s go through each step:
1. Identify your web design goals
Just like with any project, start by identifying your goals.
What are your web design goals? When do you intend to launch your newly redesigned website?
Answer these questions and clearly identify your requirements so that you have a clearer idea of what you need to look for.
2. Set a working budget
After identifying your needs, determine a budget range that fits your project. Having a web design budget helps you shortlist candidates.
Be realistic with your budget. If you want your website to have ecommerce functionalities and multiple page types, you may need a higher budget.
3. Determine whether you need a freelancer or agency
Once you’ve determined your goals and budget, you’ll have a better idea of whether you must hire a freelancer or an agency.
When to hire a freelancer
Hire a freelance web designer if you need a more affordable option or if your web design project only requires a few pages.
If you need some schedule flexibility, consider hiring a freelancer. Unlike an agency, which has employees working during office hours, freelancers are typically available to answer your questions beyond the typical business hours.
When to hire an agency
If your web design project involves several pages and features, and involves copywriting and other services, consider hiring a full-service digital marketing agency (like WebFX).
Got a tight deadline? An agency has processes in place that can manage tight deadlines and deliver your requirements on your agreed timeline.
4. Look for freelancers or agencies
Once you’ve identified whether a freelancer or agency is best for your needs, look for potential candidates.
Research online, and read reviews. Check their websites and their clients’ websites to see their work.
You can also get referrals from colleagues. Ask them about what it was like to work with these agencies to get their feedback and manage your expectations.
Once you’ve done your initial research, make a shortlist of your potential candidates based on their strengths and their published cost. We also have tips for how to find a website designer!
5. Ask your candidates for proposals
Invite your candidates to submit a proposal. Use a marketing request for proposal so that everyone has all the relevant information about your web design project.
An RFP also leads to better decision-making because you can easily compare the different freelancer and agency candidates.
6. Review the proposals
Once you’ve reviewed your candidates’ proposals, set up meetings or calls with them to discuss similar projects and ask questions. Use the opportunity to get to know the agency or freelancer better so you can gauge if they’re the right fit for your business.
7. Deliberate
Once you’ve reviewed their proposals and met with your candidates, deliberate internally on which among your web design freelancers or agencies is best for your business.
Keep your goals in mind as you choose the best web design freelancer or agency to work with. Then, once you’ve made your decision, inform your chosen freelancer or agency.
8. Finalize your contract agreement
Before signing an agreement and jumping into work together, review the contract thoroughly. Ensure the important details on the following are included:
Deliverables
Deadlines
Revisions
Costs
Payment terms
Ownership of web designs
Finalize the contract details before sealing the deal!
