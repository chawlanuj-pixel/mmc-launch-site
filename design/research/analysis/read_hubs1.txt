What is digital marketing?
Digital marketing, also called online marketing, uses online channels, electronic devices, and digital technologies to promote a business, person, product, or service to an online audience.
Digital marketing consists of strategies such as search engine optimization (SEO), content marketing, pay-per-click (PPC) advertising, social media advertising, social media marketing, email marketing, and web design. Marketing via these channels is valuable for building brand awareness, generating website traffic, attracting qualified leads, nurturing leads, driving sales, reducing customer churn, and improving overall customer satisfaction.

Digital marketing channels and strategies
Digital marketing encompasses a wide range of strategies that use online channels and digital technologies. The most common digital marketing strategies include:
Search engine optimization (SEO): SEO helps your website rank well in search results for relevant keywords which increases the quantity and quality of your website traffic.
Pay-per-click (PPC) advertising: PPC ads are online ads that appear on search engine results pages as paid search results, on social media platforms as social media ads, and on other websites as display ads. With PPC management and PPC ads, you set a budget, and your budget is only depleted based on clicks.
Social media marketing: Social media marketing is having a presence on social media platforms such as Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Pinterest. By posting regularly on social media, a company or individual can create brand awareness and engage with their audience.
Content marketing: Content marketing is the practice of creating website content to bring traffic to your site, increase brand awareness, and eventually drive leads and sales. Content types include blog posts, whitepapers, infographics, and videos.
Email marketing: Email marketing involves sending email newsletters, promotional emails, and other email campaigns to subscribers and customers. With email marketing, a company or business can nurture leads, stay top of mind, and create brand awareness.

What is SEO?
Search engine optimization (SEO) is the process of improving your website’s rankings in search results for relevant keywords to attract qualified traffic to your site. SEO involves a wide range of optimization techniques.
This strategy is organic, meaning you can’t pay to improve your rankings — you must improve your rankings through optimization.

Why is SEO important?
SEO is essential to the success of any website. If you want people to find you when they search online, you must optimize your site to rank better in search results.
Here are just a few of the many benefits of learning SEO and implementing it:
Improved visibility: When you rank higher in search results, more people will see your site and become familiar with your brand, improving your visibility and brand awareness.
Improved credibility: The higher your website ranks on search engines like Google and Bing, the more credible you appear to visitors. When you rank higher, people trust your website has the information they need, so they visit it.
Improved traffic: The higher you rank on Google, the more traffic you will get. With SEO, you can also improve the quality of your traffic by targeting specific keywords that are relevant to your audience.
Increased qualified leads and sales: SEO allows you to target keywords that are relevant to your audience and leads so you can attract potential future customers to your website. You can also rank for keywords related to your products or services.

How do search engine rankings and SEO work?
Understanding how search engines work can be helpful when you start optimizing your site for search. Here’s a brief overview and some additional resources for understanding search engines.
Search engines like Google and Bing use bots called crawlers or spiders to find and index content. These crawlers travel around the Internet through links and scan pages, adding pages to search engine’s indexes as they discover them. Once a page is part of a search engine’s index, it can appear in search results.
Search engines also assess pages to determine what they’re about and how useful they may be to users.
When a user searches something, search engines use various algorithms to determine which pages will be the most useful to the searcher and ranks them accordingly. These algorithms rank content based on more than 200 factors.
The role of SEO, then, is to optimize pages so that search engines:
Find and index your pages
Understand what your pages are about
Assess them favorably and rank them highly based on the various ranking factors
Search engines want to rank the most useful content well, so essentially, SEOs want to make content as valuable to searchers as possible.
The goal is not to trick search engines into ranking your content highly. Trying to do so can lead to penalties that lower your rankings or prevent your site from ranking entirely. Instead, the goal is to make your content useful and relevant to searchers in alignment with search engine algorithms.

Keyword research
Conducting keyword research is one of the most important things you need to do to succeed with SEO.
When researching keywords, choose terms that are relevant to your business and website and that your target audience is likely to search. You can use a keyword research tool to help you find terms to target with your content.
Most companies focus on long-tail keywords when performing research.
Long-tail keywords are phrases containing three or more words, like “women’s purple rain boots.” There is less competition for long-tail keywords, so you can rank higher in search results.
Additionally, since long-tail keywords are specific, they can drive more qualified traffic to your business, which leads to more revenue.
Short-tail keywords are shorter, broader search queries. While these keywords are more competitive, ranking well for them can bring high volumes of traffic to your site. Larger, well-established websites may be able to rank for relevant short-tail keywords.

SEO techniques to avoid
When looking at techniques to improve your SEO, you’ll come across some tactics that seem like a good idea but will hurt your website in the long run. These tactics are known as black-hat SEO techniques.
Black-hat SEO refers to strategies used to boost SEO quickly that go against search engine guidelines. Here are some techniques you must avoid:
Keyword stuffing: This refers to overloading a page with keywords in an unnatural way to try to get it to rank for those terms. Keywords should always be inserted naturally within the text and should never be overused.
Paid links: You should never pay for links, as this can hurt your website’s credibility.
Plagiarized content: Google can detect plagiarized and duplicate content. Don’t take content from other people’s websites or publish the same content repeatedly on your website, as it can impact your ranking.

What is pay-per-click (PPC) advertising?
What is PPC? If you’ve ever been on Google or Bing, you’ve probably seen it!
Pay-per-click advertising, commonly referred to as PPC, is a type of digital advertising where a company pays to have its website shown on the search engine results page (SERP). The organization then pays for each person who clicks on the ad.
How does PPC advertising work?
PPC advertising works by matching Internet users’ searches with keywords targeted by the ads.
The company then pays for each person who clicks on the ad and gets led to the company’s website. This way, your company isn’t being charged every time someone simply looks at the ad and continues to scroll.
PPC must get set up through an ad platform, like those mentioned above, and there are several requirements for creating ads. The advertiser sets a baseline bid amount, which can get adjusted later for specific keywords or search queries that they anticipate will drive revenue.

6 tips for managing a successful PPC campaign
How do you manage a successful PPC campaign?
Here are some great tips for earning more revenue from your PPC ads:
1. Conduct keyword research
In addition to completing keyword research when you first begin your campaign, it’s necessary to continue adding and removing keywords based on current trends, competitor actions, and your company’s products and services.
With this tactic, you can ensure your campaign is staying relevant with users’ searches.
2. Check your quality score
If your quality score is too low, Google and other search engines may stop it from matching with searches altogether. Check the quality of your ads every so often and work on ways to improve your ads, like by improving your landing page’s relevance to users.
3. Monitor your results
How can you improve your campaign if you’re not monitoring it? An easy way to make sure you’re getting the best results for what you’re spending is to look at your campaign data and adjust your ads based on what you see, like every week.
4. Run A/B tests
A/B testing can help you determine what is (and isn’t) working well for your PPC ads. Figure out what can be improved, make the changes, and repeat the process! This method can greatly impact the success of your PPC ads.
5. Check landing pages
Make sure your landing page is relevant to the search, and more importantly, up and running! The way users perceive your landing page is an essential factor in the success of advertising. If you don’t have a user-friendly website, your ads won’t drive revenue.
6. Add negative keywords
Negative keywords are a hidden gem of PPC advertising because they prevent your ad from displaying in searches that aren’t relevant, like for a “free” product. They can be almost as effective in the success of your PPC campaign as your targeted keywords.

What is web design?
The main focus of web design is improving your website’s aesthetic appearance and usability by creating various graphical elements. These elements include features such as infographics, ads, and logos and enhance the visual look and feel of a website. When web designers work on websites, they focus on how these elements come together to create a good user experience for site visitors.
Why is web design important?
The design of a site is the first thing any new user sees. Making a positive first impression should be a focus for any business. A clean, high-quality page will create a much better impression than a page that looks unappealing and outdated.
Another benefit of good web design is that it improves your search engine optimization or SEO. Google wants to rank sites that provide users with good experiences. Users also spend more time on well-designed websites, which sends positive signals to Google, leading to higher rankings.

Web design tips
When designing your website, there are quite a few factors to consider.
One of the main tips to remember is that your website is a part of your brand and should reflect that. Make sure that the design of your site reflects your brand since that’s how people will remember you.
You should also create a simple homepage with functional navigation. A core aspect of website design is user experience. If you overwhelm your users with too much information on the homepage and confusing navigation, they may get frustrated and opt to leave your site. Keeping your homepage simple and your navigation easy to use will keep users engaged and on your site longer.
One last key tip is ensuring that your site works well on devices of different sizes. You can achieve this with responsive design, which helps your website adapt to devices of all sizes to provide the same experience to everyone.
