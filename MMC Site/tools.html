Six calculators we use inside real engagements, plus the glossary that explains every term in them: rebuilt for you to use free. No email wall, no gated PDF, no drip sequence - put your numbers in and take the answer with you.

A number is not a verdict. Nothing here is a judgement on your brand. It shows you the shape of the problem and the order worth fixing it in, so the next quarter starts prepared instead of surprised.

CAC Runway Calculator

How long a new brand burns before it turns.

A new brand almost never hits break-even CAC on day one. This models the honest version. You overpay while the account learns, and two forces close the gap: CAC falling month on month, and repeat purchase from customers already bought. Answers the only two questions that matter: how long, and how much cash.

You get outMonths to profit · Total cash to burn · 36-month projection

Why these three, in this order

Start with Unit Economics: if one order doesn't contribute, nothing downstream matters. Then the three ROAS and retention tools answer the same question at three different altitudes. Funnel ROAS tells you where efficiency is lost. Break-even ROAS tells you what efficiency you need. LTV:CAC tells you how long you can afford to be patient about getting there.

Run in that order they compose: the funnel gives you a ROAS, break-even tells you whether that ROAS is survivable, and LTV:CAC tells you whether a thin first order is a problem or a plan. CAC Runway answers the question every new brand actually has, how long until this stops costing money, and Growth Trajectory projects whatever margin the others produce forward.

Or skip the arithmetic and let us run it with you.

A calculator tells you where the problem is. It won't tell you which of the five levers to pull first, or what it costs to move it. That's a 30-minute conversation with a senior strategist, no deck, no pitch.
