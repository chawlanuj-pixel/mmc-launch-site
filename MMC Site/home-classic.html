  1. We're a full-service growth studio. Five disciplines: design, marketing, technology, content, AI - sit in one pod for each client. Most agencies do one of these well; we built the studio so a brand never has to stitch four vendors to compound.
  2. A 6-week strategy sprint, followed by a 12-month retainer. The first 14 days are audit + roadmap. Days 15–28 are creative production and platform setup. Day 30 onwards is live media, weekly creative, monthly cohort reviews, and quarterly strategy resets.
  3. We work with brands doing ₹3Cr → ₹150Cr in annual revenue. Below that, we recommend a smaller specialist. Above that, we typically partner alongside an in-house team rather than replace it.
  4. No. About 60% of our work is D2C: beauty, food, apparel, home. The rest is healthcare, real estate, SaaS, education, and hospitality. The constant is consumer commerce: we know the physics of buying.
  5. No serious operator can. What we will guarantee is the system: weekly cadence, transparent reporting, senior strategists on every account, and a 30-day exit clause if it's not working. Our median blended ROAS across the book is 8.7×.

  9. Two weeks from kickoff to first paid media. Strategy in week 1, creative production and platform integration in week 2, live media in week 3. Most agencies need 60 days; we treat onboarding as a sprint, not a discovery phase.
  10. Book a 30-minute free brand audit. A senior strategist reads your funnel, retention, creative, and architecture, and tells you the three highest-ROI things to fix first. No pitch deck, no sales pressure. If it makes sense after, we talk retainers.
