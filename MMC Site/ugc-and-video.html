In short

UGC and video production is the work of making creator-style content that is built to run as paid ads, then testing which versions hold attention.

Monday Marketing Company (MMC) produces UGC, video and creative for D2C brands, and creative for B2B and education campaigns.

  • One brand thesisEvery video is briefed against a single idea, so tests have a direction.
  • Creators, not stockReal people talking about the product in a natural way.
  • A steady refreshNew versions arrive on a schedule, before results tire.
Where creative goes wrong

Five reasons ad creative stops working.

Creative is one of the biggest levers in paid media and one of the first to tire. Each stage below is where it goes wrong, and how we run it.

  1. 1
    Stage 1 of 5Start from one idea

    Making videos with no shared idea behind them.

    Random creative cannot be compared, so nothing is learned. A clear idea gives every new video a reason.

    What we do

    We write a brand thesis and short briefs for each video, so every test asks a clear question.

    Brand thesis and briefs
  2. 2
    Stage 2 of 5Use real people

    Polished ads that look like ads.

    Feeds tend to reward content that looks like it belongs there. Creator-style video often earns more attention than a studio spot.

    What we do

    We brief and shoot creator-style videos with real people, on location or at home, matched to your audience.

    UGC and creator shoots
  3. 3
    Stage 3 of 5Edit for the platform

    Posting the same cut everywhere.

    The first seconds decide whether anyone keeps watching, and each platform and placement has its own shape.

    What we do

    We edit each video with strong hooks and cut versions for the formats and placements you run.

    Editing for ads
  4. 4
    Stage 4 of 5Test what holds attention

    Choosing winners on opinion.

    The videos a team likes are not always the ones an audience watches. Only results show which ones hold attention.

    What we do

    We test versions against each other and read the results together with your media buying, so the next round builds on what worked.

    Creative testing
  5. 5
    Stage 5 of 5Refresh before it tires

    Running the same creative until it stops working.

    Every ad tires. A steady supply of new versions keeps results from falling off suddenly.

    What we do

    We plan a steady flow of new versions, so fresh creative is ready before the current set tires.

    Refresh cadence
What we watch

Four signals that show whether creative is working.

Read together with your cost per order or lead.

Hook rateThe share of viewers who stay past the opening seconds.
Hold rateHow far through the video people keep watching.
Cost per order or leadWhat each result costs, by creative.
Time to fatigueHow long a creative works before results fall.

Common questions

What people ask about UGC and video first.

What is UGC?

UGC means user-generated content. In advertising it usually means creator-style video with real people talking about a product, made to run as paid ads.

Do you find the creators?

We brief creators to fit your product and audience, and direct the shoot against the brand thesis.

How much creative do you make?

It depends on your ad spend and your testing plan, and we agree it after the audit call.

Do you make creative for B2B and education too?

Yes. Creative is part of the lead generation work we do for B2B and education brands, as video, statics and landing-page content.

How do you know which videos work?

We test versions against each other and read hook rate, hold rate and cost per result together with your media buying.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope and, for media, on ad spend, so we quote after the audit call, once we have seen your accounts.
