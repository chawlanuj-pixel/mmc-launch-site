In short

Social media creative is the work of planning and producing the posts, carousels and short videos that keep a brand visible and consistent where its audience spends time.

Monday Marketing Company (MMC) produces social media creatives for D2C, B2B and education brands.

  • Planned pillarsA few clear themes, so the feed says something.
  • Designed to matchPosts and carousels in one consistent visual style.
  • Works with paidThe best posts become ads, and ad results shape what we post.
Where social media goes wrong

Five reasons a brand feed does not connect.

Social works when it is planned and consistent. Each stage below is where feeds lose people, and how we run it.

  1. 1
    Stage 1 of 5Choose your pillars

    Posting whatever comes to mind.

    A feed with no themes gives followers no reason to expect anything from you.

    What we do

    We define a few content pillars tied to what your audience cares about, and plan a calendar around them.

    Content strategy
  2. 2
    Stage 2 of 5Design for the feed

    Posts that look different every time.

    Consistency is what makes a brand recognisable in a fast scroll.

    What we do

    We design posts, carousels and templates in one visual style that is recognisably yours.

    Design and carousels
  3. 3
    Stage 3 of 5Add short video

    Relying on static posts alone.

    Short vertical video is a large part of what people watch, and it gives a brand a human face.

    What we do

    We plan and produce short vertical videos, from scripts to edits, for the formats each platform favours.

    Short-form video
  4. 4
    Stage 4 of 5Keep a rhythm

    Bursts of posting followed by silence.

    Irregular posting makes a brand feel inactive and weakens the habit of following you.

    What we do

    We plan a realistic publishing rhythm that your team and ours can sustain.

    Publishing calendar
  5. 5
    Stage 5 of 5Learn what works

    Counting likes and stopping there.

    Likes do not show whether social supports the business. Reach, saves, clicks and enquiries say more.

    What we do

    We review what earns attention and action, and use it to shape the next round, including which posts to turn into ads.

    Analytics and iteration

Common questions

What people ask about social media first.

What are social media creatives?

They are the posts, carousels, stories and short videos that a brand publishes, planned around a few themes and designed in one consistent style.

Do you manage community replies?

Our work here is planning and producing the creatives. We can discuss replies and community management on the audit call.

How often do you post?

We agree a rhythm that your audience and your team can sustain, rather than a fixed number.

Do you do social for B2B and education, or only D2C?

All three. The themes and formats differ, but the method of planning, designing and learning is the same.

How does social work with paid media?

The best organic posts can be turned into ads, and paid results show which themes earn attention. See performance marketing.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope and, for media, on ad spend, so we quote after the audit call, once we have seen your accounts.
