# White-label keyword plan: changes for owner approval

Prepared 2026-10-12 from the owner's Keyword Planner export (see V2-WHITELABEL-KEYWORDS.md for method and limits). Nothing here has been applied to the site. Volumes are Keyword Planner ranges, "paid comp." is advertiser competition, not SEO difficulty, and no SERP, PAA or competitor data exists (Semrush had no API units). FAQ questions are therefore based on the sheet's FAQ-intent terms plus standard buyer intent, not on People Also Ask. Do not print volumes on the site. Rules kept: sentence-case headings, British spelling, no client counts, no claims.

## 1. Headline findings

- White-label demand sits almost entirely in SEO, link building, web development and agency terms, at 100-1k a month per market. Most other white-label services returned no data.
- The same terms are searched in India at 100-1k, but advertisers pay far more in the US and UK (top bid INR 1,400 to 3,900 versus INR 170 to 500 in India). The US/UK/Australia buyer is the commercial prize; India searches are likely Indian agencies and resellers.
- "SEO reseller" is a distinct vocabulary (100-1k in India, UK, US, Australia) and MMC's reseller page does not target it.
- "White label google ads" (IN, US 100-1k) is as large as "white label ppc" in India; the PPC page title should carry it.
- Guide-type terms ("best white label seo companies" US 100-1k) exist but are directory SERPs; do not chase with a claim page.

## 4. Merge or drop candidates

- /white-label/digital-marketing/ vs /white-label/ hub: both target the same cluster (white label digital marketing / marketing agency / services).
