# Education SEO: what was not built, and what the owner must supply

Date: 15 October 2026. Built so far: the education umbrella retarget, 8 new FAQs, Service schema, internal links, Mumbai education section and the admission marketing plan guide. Nothing below was built because MMC cannot yet stand behind the claims, or the page would be thin.

## Not built, and the facts needed

| Page | Why not built | Owner must supply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PR agency for schools or colleges | The site sells marketing, reputation (reviews) and content, not media relations. A PR page without a PR offer would be a false claim. Search odds were also low. | Whether MMC offers PR at all. If yes: the scope (press releases, media lists, placements), a named PR lead, and 2 or more real placements that can be shown. If no: leave it out for good. |
| Education reputation management page | Search demand is weak (empty autocomplete), the existing ORM service page and education pages already cover it, and a second page would duplicate them. | Only build if a real education ORM case exists: client name with written permission, what was done, dates, and what changed (a rating, review count or reply rate the client allows us to state). |
| Schools page (/industries/education/schools/) | No school client is on record. Sprouts and Bloom sell trips to schools; they are not schools. | Any school (CBSE, ICSE, IB, state board) MMC has worked with, with permission to name it. A decision on whether school work is a service MMC offers, and how Sprouts and Bloom may be described. |
| Coaching institutes page (/industries/education/coaching-institutes/) | No coaching client is on record, and demand is not quantified. | Any coaching institute MMC has worked with and may name, plus confirmation MMC wants this audience. |

## Reviews and listings to set up (off-site, drives "best education marketing company in India")

The "best ..." terms are won by being named on lists and directories, not by our own page. Set up and keep NAP (name, address, phone) identical everywhere.

- Clutch (education industry filter): profile plus 3 to 5 genuine client reviews, at least one from MVJ.
- GoodFirms and DesignRush (education category).
- Semrush Agencies (education and training, India).
- The Manifest: a profile already appears in the index; claim it and check what it says.
- Google Business Profile: add education services to the profile and ask for reviews.
- Then pitch independent list publishers (not competitor agency blogs) with a factual brand sheet.

Trade-press pitch idea (needs a real, approved story): an original, factual piece on tracking an enquiry through to an enrolment (the four numbers: cost per qualified enquiry, enquiry-to-application rate, cost per enrolment, response time), for afaqs, exchange4media, Agency Reporter or Education World. No statistics unless MMC has its own data it may publish.
