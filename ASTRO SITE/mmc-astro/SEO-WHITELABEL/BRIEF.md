# BRIEF (owner's words, saved verbatim in structure): Keyword research, SEO silos and organic acquisition for the MMC white-label site

Objective: attract qualified AGENCY BUYERS seeking white-label fulfilment and outsourced delivery partners, not consumers seeking general digital marketing. Core success metric: organic-sourced qualified white-label agency partnership opportunities, not traffic.

1. KEYWORD RESEARCH: US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Singapore, India. Sources: Google Keyword Planner, Search Console, Ahrefs, Semrush, autocomplete, related searches, live SERPs. Minimum 200 candidate keywords plus a refined shortlist. Classify across: white-label agency and partnership intent; white-label service fulfilment; marketing agency outsourcing; dedicated delivery teams; offshore agency outsourcing; white-label tech and web development; specialist marketing execution; agency operations and fulfilment challenges; agency partner comparisons; country-specific commercial searches; transactional and partnership searches; informational queries with a credible path to conversion. Per keyword: exact keyword; country and language; intent; monthly volume where available; difficulty or competitive indicator; CPC where available; SERP characteristics; primary ICP; commercial relevance score; organic conversion potential; existing ranking URL if any; recommended target URL; priority and rationale. NEVER invent volumes, difficulty, CPC or existing rankings. Mark unavailable measures "unverified" and record source and date of each verified one.
2. INTENT AND SERP ANALYSIS per priority cluster: what Google prefers (service pages, partnership pages, directories, articles, comparisons), common structures, depth, buyer questions, proof needed, SERP features and AI-answer chances, gaps MMC can fill with real operational expertise. Say which keywords share one page and which need their own. No one URL per keyword.
3. KEYWORD-TO-PAGE MAP for existing and proposed white-label site. Per URL: primary keyword, secondary cluster, intent, ICP, market, SEO title (practical SERP limits), meta description, H1, H2/H3 structure, supporting content, FAQ topics, internal links, schema types, CTA. Identify every cannibalisation and recommend consolidate, differentiate or redirect. Preserve existing URLs where possible.
4. SILO ARCHITECTURE beneath /white-label/: Pillar (white-label digital marketing services for agencies); Service fulfilment (SEO, PPC, paid social, web development, Shopify, social, creative, retention, GEO/AEO, relevant tech); Operations and delivery (agency outsourcing, dedicated teams, onboarding, QA, reporting, SLAs where documented, pricing models, reseller partnerships); ICP solutions (specialist agencies, creative studios, web studios, other validated segments); International delivery (India-based outsourcing, justified country pages); Educational content (outsourcing economics, hiring vs fulfilment, processes, partner selection, scaling delivery, comparisons). Per cluster: parent, children, cross-links, anchor text strategy, intent. Do not force a URL hierarchy needing unnecessary redirects.
5. NEW PAGE OPPORTUNITIES by comparing current coverage, existing rankings and impressions, competitor coverage, intent, commercial relevance, incremental demand. Candidates: digital marketing fulfilment services; white-label marketing agency India; outsourced SEO fulfilment; PPC fulfilment for agencies; white-label Shopify development; dedicated agency delivery teams; white-label marketing for creative agencies; for web development agencies. Recommend a page only with distinct intent, useful original content and plausible acquisition. Prioritised content gap report.
6. INTERNATIONAL SEO country by country: demand differences; per market decide global page vs localized page, country proof and FAQs, terminology, international case studies, regional CTAs. hreflang only when justified. No automatic page per country.
7. AEO AND GEO: direct concise answers, clear service definitions, delivery workflows, original operational insight, evidence-based comparisons, transparent capability boundaries, author and business identity, accurate structured data matching visible content, crawlable content, consistent entities. Never imply schema guarantees rich results or AI citations.
8. TECHNICAL SEO AUDIT of the Astro implementation and deployed site: sitemap, robots.txt, indexability, canonicals, duplicates, redirect chains, internal links, navigation, breadcrumbs, structured data, meta tags, heading hierarchy, Core Web Vitals, mobile performance, JS rendering, images, accessibility, orphans, 404s, pagination, cross-links between white-label and direct client service pages. Separate errors, warnings, opportunities. Check historical organic performance and backlinks (where accessible) before recommending redirects or consolidation.
9. 90-DAY CONTENT ROADMAP: commercial landing pages before low-intent blog volume. Per item: topic, primary and secondary keywords, intent, ICP, URL, outline, internal links, unique information required, effort, priority, conversion role. Comparison and educational content only where it helps real purchase decisions.
10. MEASUREMENT PLAN using Search Console, GA4 and the MMC CRM: impressions, non-branded visibility, clicks, landing-page conversion rate, white-label enquiries, qualified agency leads, meetings booked, proposals requested, opportunities, closed-won partnerships, organic-sourced pipeline value. Separate lead volume from ICP-qualified demand. Explain how to measure change without crediting everything to SEO.
