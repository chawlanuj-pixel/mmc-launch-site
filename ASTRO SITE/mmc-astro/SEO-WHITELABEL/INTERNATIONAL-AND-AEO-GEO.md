### Gate for any country page (for example /white-label/uk/)
All of: (1) 3 or more qualified agency leads from that country in the CRM within a quarter; (2) at least one client or case study there that may be named; (3) a country-specific commercial fact (currency billing, contract entity or local hours); (4) Search Console shows country-modified queries landing on global pages at positions 8 to 20. Then one country page with at least 50 percent unique text. Competitors that use country folders (for example a /white-label/uk/ URL seen in the US SERP sample on 22 Oct 2026) are not evidence that MMC should.

### hreflang
Not justified today: there is one English version of each page and no alternates. The current self-referencing `en-IN` + `x-default` pair adds nothing. Recommendation in TECHNICAL-AUDIT.md (W3). Add hreflang only if a genuine alternate page (for example an en-AE edition with different pricing and content) is ever built.

### Country proof and international case studies
None recorded in the repo for white-label work. Do not state client counts, partner names, results or awards. What can be published truthfully now: the NDA, the no-contact rule, the four onboarding stages, the reporting format, the delivery cities (Mumbai and Indore per the site), and illustrative sample artefacts marked as illustrative.

## 3. AEO and GEO plan (answer engines and AI assistants)

What is documented practice versus hypothesis is marked. No markup or tactic below is promised to produce rich results or AI citations.

### 3.1 Direct answers (documented practice: answer-first structure is easier to extract)
Every white-label commercial page opens with a 40 to 60 word "In short" answer that names MMC and answers the primary query literally. Required answers:

| Page | Question the answer block must answer |
|---|---|
| /white-label/ | What is a white-label digital marketing agency, and what does MMC do as one? |
| /white-label/seo/ | What is white-label SEO and what does it include? |
| /white-label/reseller-programme/ | What is an SEO reseller programme and how does reselling work with MMC? |
| /white-label/ppc/ | What is white-label PPC, and whose ad account is used? |
| /white-label/india/ | What does a white-label agency in India do for agencies abroad, and how are hours handled? |
| /white-label/outsourcing/ | What is agency outsourcing with a dedicated team? |
| /white-label/pricing-models/ | How is white-label work priced? |

### 3.2 Clear service definitions and capability boundaries
Each service page gets two short lists: "What is included" and "What we do not do" (for example: no paid link networks, no ranking guarantees, no contact with your client unless asked, no ad spend paid through MMC unless agreed). Boundaries are citable and reduce bad-fit leads.

### 3.3 Delivery workflow as structured, original content
MMC already documents a four-stage onboarding (NDA and scope, pilot client, SOPs, scale) and per-service "five places delivery slips" diagrams. Turn these into numbered steps with named outputs ("A written scope and an access checklist"). This is the operational insight competitors' generic pages lack.

### 3.4 Evidence-based comparisons
Use tables, not claims: reseller vs referral vs white label; dedicated team vs pooled hours vs project; platform vs specialist partner vs hire. Base cells on how the models work, not on invented costs.

### 3.5 Author and business identity
- Organisation facts must agree everywhere. Today: the site says "in content and media since 2015, a full-service growth agency since 2020" and JSON-LD `foundingDate` is 2020; `SEO-SITE-ARCHITECTURE.md` says founded 2019; The Manifest profile (seen 22 Oct 2026) says founded 2015 and calls MMC "a small branding company"; a G2 listing for "Monday Marketing" (monday-marketing.com, founded 2008) is a different business. Fix the profiles and add `sameAs` only for profiles MMC controls.
- A named reviewer for white-label guides (owner said founder bylines are not necessary; an organisation author is acceptable).

### 3.6 Structured data that matches visible content
Service (with `areaServed` and `audience` corrected), BreadcrumbList, FAQPage (questions identical to the visible FAQ), Article on guides, `hasOfferCatalog` on the hub. Validate with the house `schema-check` tooling in V2 or Google's Rich Results Test.

### 3.7 Crawlable content and consistent entities
Content is static HTML already. Keep the robots.txt AI policy explicit (it is). Keep `llms.txt` listing white-label pages, and add a one-line definition per service plus the onboarding stages (hypothesis: low-cost, may help some assistants; unproven).

### 3.8 Third-party presence (what assistants and directory SERPs read)
SERP samples on 22 Oct 2026 for India and fulfilment queries showed Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Techreviewer and MarketingMonk profiles. Claim or update MMC's profiles with a white-label service line and identical entity facts. Reviews only from real partners. Unverified whether MMC has profiles on each; owner to confirm.
