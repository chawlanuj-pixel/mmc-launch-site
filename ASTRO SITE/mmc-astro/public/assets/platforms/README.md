# Platform marks

(`home26/partners.jsx`). Each is the vendor's own artwork, downloaded on
2026-08-29 from the source below, not a redrawing of it.

The marks that previously shipped here were hand-drawn approximations, and
several were wrong outright: the "Google Ads" mark was Google's own G, Razorpay
was an invented wedge, Klaviyo an invented triangle.

## Why some are PNG

Simple Icons has removed Amazon, LinkedIn, Adobe and Tableau at those companies'
request, so those four came from the vendors' own sites. GoKwik, Shopflo and
MoEngage publish no SVG mark, so their app icons are used at 192-256px, which
is comfortably over the 26px the slot renders at.

`tableau.png` is the only file that was altered: Tableau ships a horizontal
lockup, and the slot is square, so the leading symbol was cropped out of the
official SVG at 600px tall. No pixel was redrawn.

## Two things that are not logo problems

**BuySpeed** has no file here. Nothing by that name exists in the Indian D2C
checkout market the rest of that group describes; `buyspeed.com` is US
procurement software. It keeps a neutral placeholder in `P_FALLBACK` until the
intended platform is confirmed. Breeze, Shiprocket Checkout and Razorpay Magic
Checkout are the likely candidates.

**Adobe Analytics** shows Adobe's corporate mark. Adobe does not publish a
standalone Analytics icon outside its product UI, so the parent brand stands in.

## Usage

These are third-party trademarks, shown to say which platforms MMC operates.
That is ordinary nominative use, but the "Partner" and "API" badges are claims
about a business relationship with Meta, Google, Shopify and WhatsApp, and each
of those programmes has its own rules about displaying partner status. Confirm
those badges are accurate before launch.
