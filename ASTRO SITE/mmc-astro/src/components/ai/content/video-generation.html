The short answer

AI video generation is making video from scripts, images and prompts instead of cameras and sets. MMC writes the concept, builds the shots with AI tools, edits them together with sound, and delivers cuts sized for Reels, Shorts, connected TV and your site. A senior editor reviews every frame.

How it works

Four steps from brief to live.

  1. Brief and script

    We turn your product and goal into a short script and shot list.

  2. Generate the shots

    Each scene is generated, then checked for realism, brand colour and product accuracy.

  3. Edit and sound

    Cut, colour, add music, voice-over and captions.

  4. Deliver every size

    Vertical, square and wide versions for every placement.

Common questions

Before you ask.

What kinds of video can AI make for us?

Product films, ad hooks, explainers, mascot animations and stylised brand stories. Live footage of real people is a different job and we will tell you when a real shoot is the better fit.

Do you use our real product?

Yes. We build from your product images and check every shot against them.

Can videos be made in different languages?

Voice-over and captions can be produced in the languages your audience uses. We confirm the language list when we scope the work.

Who owns the video?

Ownership and usage terms are agreed in the project scope.

Can you run the ads too?

Yes. Video sits inside our performance loop, so hooks are tested and reported on.
