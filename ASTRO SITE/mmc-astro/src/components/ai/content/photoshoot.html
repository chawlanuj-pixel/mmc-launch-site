Common questions

Before you ask.

What do you need from us to start?

One clear, high-resolution photo of the product, person or place, plus a list of the scenes you want.

Will the product still look like our product?

We start from your real photo and check every scene against it. Anything that drifts from the real product is corrected or dropped before you see it.

Can we use the images in paid ads?

Yes. We format the files for ad platforms, marketplaces and your website. Check each platform's current rules on AI-generated imagery, and we will flag anything relevant.

How long does it take?

It depends on the number of scenes and the rounds of changes. We confirm the timing when we scope the work.

Do you also run the ads?

We can. AI by MMC sits next to our performance marketing, so images can go straight into testing and reporting.
