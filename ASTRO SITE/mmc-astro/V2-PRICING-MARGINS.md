# Pricing margins (internal)

Internal only. Not in public/ or src/pages. Generated by `node tools/pricing-margin-check.mjs --md` from `src/data/pricing/*`.

How the test works: cost is the same India team hour whoever the client is, so there is ONE floor, Rs.800 per logged man-hour (a stand-in for MMC's loaded cost per hour; replace it in `margin-config.json` once the true figure and minimum margin are known). A US$ price is converted at fxInrPerUsd (85, a conservative assumption) and tested against the same rupee floor, so the dollar floor is about $9.41 an hour. Yields below are all in rupees per logged hour, so India and International are directly comparable.

List prices on the India and International pages are the owner's, with one decision applied (soc-community India Rs.15,000 to Rs.18,500, see V2-PRICING-DECISIONS.md). Camera assistant, camera and lighting are pass-through crew or equipment rates, not billable man-hours, and are excluded from the yield check. Set `enforceFloorOnClientPrices: true` to lift any client price under the floor.

There are four audiences and four private pages: India, International (US$), White-label India (factor 0.70) and White-label International (factor 0.75). White-label rule: list x factor, rounded, then lifted to floor x hours but never above list; a line already under the floor at list would be THIN (white-label equals list). Result: no THIN lines on any audience. The audit bundle (20% off the combined price) applies on all four pages and stays above the floor in both currencies. "Agency margin at list resale" is 1 minus white-label price over list price: what an agency keeps if it resells at the client list price.

One cost floor for every audience: Rs.800 per logged man-hour (a stand-in for loaded cost). US$ prices are tested at price x Rs.85 per $ against it, which makes the dollar floor $9.41 an hour. White-label factor 0.7 (INR) and 0.75 (USD). enforceFloorOnClientPrices: false.
Yield is price divided by the logged hours of one priced unit (per word for content, per keyword for SEO, per influencer for gifting, per creator for paid influencer minimums). Excluded from the check as pass-through rates, not billable man-hours: shoot-camera, shoot-light, shoot-assist. Client price overrides applied: {"soc-community":{"inr":18500}}.
