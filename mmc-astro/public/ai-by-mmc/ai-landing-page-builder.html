The short answer

An AI landing page builder generates page copy and layouts from a brief, so many pages can be made and tested in the time one used to take. MMC uses it to produce pages per campaign, then refines them by hand, connects forms and analytics, and reports on which pages convert.

How it works

Four steps from brief to live.

  1. Discover

    We read your offer, audience and the ad that sends people here.

  2. Define

    We agree the promise, proof, structure and one action for the page.

  3. Generate

    AI drafts copy and layouts. We choose the strongest directions.

  4. Refine and launch

    Editing, speed checks, tracking and live launch.
