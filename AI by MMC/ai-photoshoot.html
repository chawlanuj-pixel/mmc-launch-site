How it works

Four steps from brief to live.

  1. Send the base photo

    One clear photo of the product, person or place. Your real subject stays the anchor.

  2. Choose the scenes

    Tell us where the brand lives: kitchen, street, campus, festival, studio. We propose a scene list.

  3. We generate and refine

    Scenes are built, checked against your real product, and corrected until they look right.

  4. Receive channel-ready files

    Square, vertical and wide crops for ads, marketplaces and the website.
